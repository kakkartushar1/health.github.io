#12 × 12 • Rest 60–90 sec

Squat

Why: Primary lower-body compound movement.

Targets: Quads, glutes, hamstrings, core

Starting weight: 20 kg empty bar if comfortable, otherwise a lighter variation
Verified exercise-form photo
GIF: No verified GIF selected. A wrong GIF is worse than no GIF.
Open form video ↗

How to do it

  1. Set the bar safely in a rack across the upper back.
  2. Feet about shoulder-width, toes slightly out. Brace before each rep.
  3. Bend hips and knees together and descend under control.
  4. Use the deepest comfortable range where balance, foot contact and spinal control remain stable.
  5. Drive through the mid-foot and stand tall.

Breathing: Inhale and brace before descent. Exhale through the hardest part of the ascent.

Tempo: 2–3 sec down, controlled reversal, 1–2 sec up.

Key cues: Heels planted • knees track toes • brace • bar over mid-foot

Common mistakes: Knees collapsing inward, heels lifting, uncontrolled depth, losing the brace.

Stop/reduce: Sharp pain, loss of balance, or inability to keep technique.

#22 × 10–12 • Rest 60–90 sec

Romanian Deadlift

Why: Posterior-chain hinge for hamstrings and glutes.

Targets: Hamstrings, glutes, erectors, core, grip

Starting weight: 15–20 kg barbell OR 7.5–10 kg dumbbells in each hand
Verified exercise-form photo
GIF: No verified GIF selected. A wrong GIF is worse than no GIF.
Open form video ↗

How to do it

  1. Stand hip-width with the weight against the thighs. Keep knees softly bent.
  2. Brace your abdomen and keep ribs stacked over pelvis.
  3. Push hips straight backward. Think: close a car door with your hips.
  4. Keep the bar/dumbbells close to the legs.
  5. Descend only until the hamstrings are strongly stretched while the spine remains neutral.
  6. Drive hips forward and squeeze glutes to stand tall.

Breathing: Inhale and brace before the hinge. Keep the brace through the rep and exhale as you stand.

Tempo: 2–3 sec down, brief pause, 1–2 sec up.

Key cues: Hips back • soft knees • weight close • neutral spine

Common mistakes: Squatting down, rounding the back, weight drifting forward, forcing excessive range.

Stop/reduce: Back starts rounding, sharp back pain, numbness/tingling, or lower back becomes the dominant sensation.

Good hamstring stretch = strong tension along the back of the thighs while your trunk stays stable. Lower-back loading = pressure becomes dominant or your spine changes shape. Reduce range or weight. Do not lower farther simply to reach the floor.
#32 × 12 • Rest 60–90 sec

Leg Press

Why: Stable compound movement for additional quad and glute volume.

Targets: Quads, glutes, hamstrings

Starting weight: 40–60 kg plate load as a starting test range
Verified exercise-form photo
GIF: No verified GIF selected. A wrong GIF is worse than no GIF.
Open form video ↗

How to do it

  1. Sit with back and head supported. Feet about hip-width on the platform.
  2. Brace and release the safety mechanism according to your machine.
  3. Lower under control without letting the pelvis roll off the pad.
  4. Press through the whole foot, knees aligned with toes.
  5. Stop short of a hard knee lockout.

Breathing: Inhale while lowering. Exhale while pressing.

Tempo: 2 sec down, controlled press, no bounce.

Key cues: Hips stay down • knees follow toes • controlled depth • no hard lockout

Common mistakes: Too much weight, hips lifting, knees collapsing inward, bouncing.

Stop/reduce: Pelvis lifts, knee pain appears, or you cannot control the platform.

Leg Press Calf Raise

Why: Your final replacement for the unavailable calf-raise machine.

Targets: Gastrocnemius, soleus

Starting weight: 30–40 kg as a light test range
Verified exercise-form photo
GIF: No verified GIF selected. A wrong GIF is worse than no GIF.
Open form video ↗

How to do it

  1. Use the leg press with back and head supported and the safety mechanism correctly set.
  2. Place only the balls of your feet on the lower portion of the platform, heels beyond the edge.
  3. Keep knees stable and nearly straight.
  4. Lower heels slowly into a comfortable stretch.
  5. Raise heels as high as comfortable and pause.
  6. Repeat without bouncing.

Breathing: Breathe normally. Avoid prolonged breath holding.

Tempo: 2 sec down, brief stretch, 1 sec up, brief squeeze.

Key cues: Ankles move • knees stable • controlled range • no bouncing

Common mistakes: Too much weight, bending knees, bouncing, insecure foot placement.

Stop/reduce: Platform becomes unstable, heel control is lost, or knee/Achilles pain occurs.

#51–2 × 12 • Rest 45–60 sec

Leg Extension

Why: Quad isolation after the compound work.

Targets: Quadriceps

Starting weight: 15–20 kg
Verified exercise-form photo
GIF: No verified GIF selected. A wrong GIF is worse than no GIF.
Open form video ↗

How to do it

  1. Align knee with the machine pivot.
  2. Set ankle pad comfortably on the lower shin.
  3. Keep back against pad and hold handles.
  4. Extend knees smoothly and pause near the top.
  5. Lower slowly without dropping the stack.

Breathing: Exhale while extending. Inhale while lowering.

Tempo: 1–2 sec up, 1 sec squeeze, 2–3 sec down.

Key cues: Knee aligned • smooth reps • no swing • controlled negative

Common mistakes: Kicking the weight, leaning back, lifting hips, excessive load.

Stop/reduce: Sharp knee pain or loss of control.

#62 × 12 • Rest 45–60 sec

Stability Ball Hamstring Curl

Why: Your replacement for the broken leg-curl machine.

Targets: Hamstrings, glutes, core

Starting weight: Bodyweight
Verified exercise-form photo
GIF: No verified GIF selected. A wrong GIF is worse than no GIF.
Open form video ↗

How to do it

  1. Lie on your back with heels on the stability ball.
  2. Brace and lift hips into a bridge.
  3. Curl the ball toward the glutes while keeping hips elevated.
  4. Pause briefly.
  5. Extend legs slowly and return under control.

Breathing: Exhale while curling. Inhale while extending.

Tempo: 1–2 sec curl, brief squeeze, 2–3 sec return.

Key cues: Hips up • heels pull • ribs down • slow return

Common mistakes: Hips dropping, over-arching the back, rolling the ball too fast.

Stop/reduce: Repeated cramping or lower-back discomfort. Reset and reduce range.
