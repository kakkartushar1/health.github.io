About Yin Yoga

Yin yoga targets the deep connective tissues — ligaments, fascia and joints — rather than the muscles. Poses are held passively for longer periods, typically 2–5 minutes each.

It complements the more active workouts during the week by improving joint mobility, releasing tension and encouraging recovery. Sunday is an ideal day for this slower practice.

General Tips

  • Hold each pose for the full duration shown in the video. Yin is about stillness and time.
  • Find your edge — a moderate stretch sensation — then stop. Do not push into pain.
  • Muscles should be relatively relaxed. Let gravity and time do the work.
  • Use props (bolster, blanket, blocks) to make longer holds comfortable.
  • Breathe slowly and naturally. Deep belly breathing helps you settle in.
  • Feeling stiff when you come out of a pose is normal. Move slowly between poses.
  • This is recovery, not performance. There is no "correct" depth for any pose.
