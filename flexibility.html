General Tips

  • Follow the video at your own pace; pause if you need more time in a stretch.
  • Breathe steadily throughout. Never hold your breath during a stretch.
  • Mild tension is expected; sharp pain means you should ease off.
  • Use a mat for comfort. A towel or yoga block can help if flexibility is limited.
  • Stay hydrated before, during and after the session.
  • Consistency matters more than depth. Regular practice improves flexibility over time.
